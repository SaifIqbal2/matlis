Annali di Igiene, Medicina Preventiva e di Comunità is an international, multidisciplinary, full open access and peer-reviewed journal. It publishes original researches, reviews, rapid public health reports, and commentaries in the fields of preventive medicine, environmental health, and health policy.

Annali di Igiene, Medicina Preventiva e di Comunità reaches 2025 Journal Impact Factor of 2.4

2026-06-18

We are pleased to announce that Annali di Igiene, Medicina Preventiva e di Comunità has received a 2025 Journal Impact Factor of 2.4, according to the latest Journal Citation Reports™.

This result represents the journal’s highest Impact Factor to date and marks an important achievement for Annali di Igiene within the fields of hygiene, preventive medicine, public health, and community medicine.

The journal has entered Q2 in the Journal Citation Reports™ / WoS category Health Care Sciences & Services, marking an important milestone in its growing international visibility. In Scopus/SCImago, Annali di Igiene is ranked Q2 in the categories Infectious Diseases and Public Health, Environmental and Occupational Health.

We would like to thank the Editors-in-Chief, Editorial Board members, reviewers, authors, and readers for their continued commitment and support. This achievement reflects a shared effort to promote rigorous, relevant, and high-quality research in hygiene, preventive medicine, public health, and community health.

We look forward to continuing this path of growth and scientific contribution in the coming years.
