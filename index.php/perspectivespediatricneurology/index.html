Official Journal of the Scientific Committee of the Italian Society of Pediatric Neurology (SINP). The Italian Society of Pediatric Neurology is affiliated to the Italian Society of Pediatrics (SIP). Under the guidance of a prestigious national and international editorial board, this multi-disciplinary journal publishes exciting clinical and experimental research in this rapidly expanding field. High quality papers written by leading experts encompass all the major diseases including developmental neurobiology and nervous system malformations,  foetal and neonatal neurology, pediatric neurophysiology and neuroimaging, epilepsy and epileptic syndromes, neurometabolic disorders, neuromuscular disorders, neurocutaneous diseases, movement disorders, neurodegenerative disorders, neurodevelopmental disorders, and intellectual disability. Other exciting highlights include articles on basic neurosciences, and the publication of regularly updated tables relating to the main groups of disorders.
