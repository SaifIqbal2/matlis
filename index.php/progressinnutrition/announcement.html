  • Proofreading request

    12-06-2019

    Please pay attention to these instructions: the corrections to the articles will be accepted and the articles will be corrected when and only, we will send you the official letter of proofreading request. The submitted corrections not requested by the Editorial Office will not be considered. We will send you the proofreading request about 1 month before the publication of the article.

  • New Policy - New publication fee starting from 2019

    29-12-2018

    Starting from 1-1-2019, all submitted articles will have to pay a fee of 500 euro (for the articles submitted before 1st January 2019 the fee will be 250 euro). The fee should be paid after the acceptation of the article and before the publication.  Instructions are given in the Journal’s Guidelines. The fee is due for all the articles submitted. The new policy doesn't provide any waves or discounts. The discount/waiver requests will not be answered.

    Read More Read more about New Policy - New publication fee starting from 2019

  • Submission alert

    19-04-2018

    The submission form must contain the full names of ALL authors, using the button "ADD CONTRIBUTORS" with capital letters only for the first letter of authors’ names. If the submission has missed the co-authors name, it is not possible to add them later and THE SUBMISSION MUST BE REPEATED.
