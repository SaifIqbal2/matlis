  • THANK YOU MESSAGE FROM THE EDITOR-IN-CHIEF

    15-12-2025

    On behalf of Acta Biomedica, I would like to express my deepest appreciation and sincere gratitude to the Editors, all Editorial Board Members, and Reviewers for their invaluable advice, guidance, and essential role in maintaining the high scientific quality of the research published in each issue of the Journal. I also wish to thank the many Researchers whose tireless efforts and dedication have made these achievements possible. It has been a great pleasure to work with you and to present your work.

    At the time of publication of the sixth and final issue of 2025, Acta Biomedica received a total of 475 manuscripts, with an overall rejection rate of 57%, reflecting our continued commitment to scientific rigor and quality.

    Acta Biomedica is pleased with the progress achieved throughout 2025 and warmly encourages Researchers to submit original and high-quality scientific contributions to further enhance the Journal’s quality and impact in the coming year.

    In closing, I would like to thank the readers of Acta Biomedica for their continued interest and support, as well as the editorial office for its invaluable and professional assistance.

    Thank you for being part of this vibrant scientific community. I wish you a year filled with new opportunities and continued success.

    Sincerely,

    Maurizio Vanelli
    Editor-in-Chief
    Acta Biomedica

  • Submission alert and revised manuscript

    02-10-2018

    The submission form must contain the full names of ALL authors, using the button "ADD CONTRIBUTORS" with capital letters only for the first letter of authors’ names. If the submission has missed the co-authors name, it is not possible to add them later and THE SUBMISSION MUST BE REPEATED.

    When revising your manuscript, please show corrections by using track changes or highlighting them in red color. Be sure to respond to all reviewer comments including also a letter with your comments about. You should upload two files: - Revised manuscript file with changes highlighted or in red colour. In Word you can use Track Changes, then Highlight Changes or simply Underline your changes or use the red colour. Use a short file name, such as revised (the file extension should be added automatically), for your new manuscript file. - File containing response to reviewers.
