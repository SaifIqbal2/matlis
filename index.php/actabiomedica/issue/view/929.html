FROM THE EDITOR-IN-CHIEF

Acta Biomedica (ABM) would like to thank all the reviewers who have generously dedicated their time and expertise to ensuring the quality of the research published in each issue of ABM. We also thank all the authors for their kind collaboration during the various stages of manuscript development and the associate editors and editorial board for their advice and guidance on manuscripts submitted for publication.

Acta Biomedica is pleased with the progress made in 2024 and looks forward to the support of authors, reviewers and readers to face the new year's challenges.

Finally, I would like to thank the readers of Acta Biomedica for their interest in the journal and encourage all of them to send us their valuable feedback and ideas for further improvement of our journal.

Sincerely yours.

Maurizio Vanelli
Editor in Chief of Acta Biomedica
