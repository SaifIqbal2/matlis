Open Access Policy and Article Processing Charges (APC)

In open access publishing, the full texts of published articles are freely available to readers worldwide without delay. Publishing an article in this open access journal is subject to an Article Processing Charge (APC), which covers the journal’s scientific and editorial costs. These include article production, editorial services, technical infrastructure, innovation, electronic archiving (from 2003 onward), and customer service.

 

APC Details

The following publication fees apply:

  • Original articles, Reviews, Case reports: €500 (VAT included)
  • Letters to the Editor: €200 (VAT included)

These fees cover the costs of editorial work, editing, publication (online and print), and long-term archiving.

The corresponding author is responsible for the payment of the APC, either personally or through their institution (or that of a co-author). The APC must be paid after acceptance of the article but before final publication.

Accepted papers will be published according to the current publication queue of Acta Biomedica (i.e., in the next available issue, based on payment order and space availability).

 

Fast Publication Option

A fast-track process may be recommended for:

  • High scientific relevance or timely topic (e.g., emerging evidence, rapidly evolving field).
  • Author-related timing needs, such as project dissemination requirements, grant deadlines, or academic evaluation timelines.
  • Potential for increased visibility, as accelerated publication may support earlier readership and citations.
  • Recommendation by the Editorial Board due to scientific or strategic interest.

Acceptance the request is the responsability of the Editorial Board.

Authors requesting fast publication may opt for an additional service at a fee of €1000 (VAT included). This fee reflects the additional editorial management workload and ensures that the article will be published in the next available forthcoming issue of Acta Biomedica.

 

Reduced APC Eligibility

A special fee of € 250 (VAT included) is applyed only to:

- Authors from low-income countries (according to World Bank Open Data).

 

Waivers and Free Submissions

The following types of submissions are exempt from APCs:

  • Correspondence to the Editor (NOT Letters to Editor)
  • Commentaries and Viewpoints (by invitation)
  • PhD students, medical residents, and other young health professionals (under 30 years of age):
    Authors may submit a maximum of two manuscripts per year, provided that all submissions comply with the aims and author guidelines of Acta Biomedica. Only Original Articles reporting significant and original contributions to scientific knowledge in the medical disciplines will be considered for publication. A maximum of four students may be included among the authors. To ensure appropriate scientific guidance, each manuscript must be supported by mandatory mentorship throughout the writing and submission process. The mentor must be listed as a co-author of the manuscript. Manuscripts accepted for publication are exempt from the Article Processing Charge (APC).

 

Final Proof and “Online First” Publication

Before final publication, authors will receive a final proof from the editorial office for review. Once the proof has been approved and the APC has been paid, the article will be published in the “Online First” section of the journal website. This allows early visibility prior to its assignment to a regular issue.

 

Withdrawal Policy

Authors retain the right to withdraw their article at any stage of the editorial process. However, withdrawals requested after the start of editorial assessment and/or peer review, including after acceptance, will incur a fee equal to 50% of the APC.

For questions regarding APCs, waivers, or manuscript withdrawal, please contact the Managing Editor, Dr. Valeria Ceci ([email protected])

 

Misconduct, Plagiarism, and AI-related Violations

Authors are fully responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of all references included in their manuscripts. In cases where serious inconsistencies are found—such as non-existent references, fabricated data, or citation of unpublished material—the editorial office reserves the right to suspend or terminate the editorial process at any stage.

The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the preparation of references must be explicitly declared under the Declarations section of the manuscript. The editorial office may conduct verifications both before and after pre-acceptance by the Editor-in-Chief. Manuscripts found to include AI-generated references without disclosure may be subject to immediate suspension.

In the event of confirmed misconduct, including but not limited to plagiarism, fabrication, or fraudulent citation practices, no refund will be granted for any editorial or publication services already rendered. Furthermore, if the manuscript is withdrawn due to misconduct after acceptance or final processing, the publisher reserves the right to apply a penalty fee equivalent to twice the standard publication fee.

 

APC Payment and Withdrawal Policy

By submitting a manuscript to the Journal, the corresponding author confirms that all authors have read and accepted the Journal's publication policies, including the applicable Article Processing Charge (APC), where relevant.

Submission of a manuscript constitutes acceptance of the obligation to pay the applicable APC upon acceptance of the article, unless a waiver or discount has been formally approved by the Publisher before submission or during the initial editorial assessment.

Authors requesting an APC waiver or discount must do so before manuscript submission or, at the latest, before the manuscript enters peer review. Requests submitted after peer review, acceptance, or production has commenced will normally not be considered.

If, after acceptance, the authors decide not to proceed with publication for reasons unrelated to editorial decisions, or fail to fulfil the agreed publication terms, the Publisher reserves the right to request reimbursement of the editorial and production costs already incurred. These costs may include, but are not limited to:

  • editorial management;
  • peer-review administration;
  • scientific editorial assessment;
  • copy-editing;
  • typesetting and production preparation;
  • administrative processing.

In such cases, the Publisher may require payment of a withdrawal or cost-recovery fee, determined in accordance with the stage reached by the manuscript in the publication process and the actual editorial services already provided.

The manuscript will not be formally released for submission elsewhere until all applicable contractual and administrative obligations have been fulfilled and written confirmation of release has been issued by the Publisher.

Nothing in this policy limits the authors' rights to withdraw a manuscript before acceptance, subject to the stage of editorial processing and any applicable contractual obligations.

 

When and how to pay

Upon editorial acceptance of an article, the corresponding author will be notified that payment is due. We advise prompt payment as we are unable to publish accepted articles until full payment has been received. Payment can be made by one of the following methods:

  1. PayPal/Credit cards – These are the most recommended and secure payment systems, enabling you to pay without sharing your financial information and getting your payment receipt immediately.
  2. Bank transfer - Payment is due within 30 days of the manuscript receiving editorial acceptance. Bank charges are to be borne by the payer. Please enclose a copy of the bank receipt. The article ID and journal name must be clearly stated in the payment reference. Failure to provide this information will prevent the payment from being matched to the corresponding article, which will therefore not be validated.
